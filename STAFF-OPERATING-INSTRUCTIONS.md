# Staff operating instructions

## Creating a client link

1. Open `/admin` and enter the staff access key.
2. **Choose the questionnaire first:**
   - *FOS questionnaire only* — the client answers the FOS questions.
   - *Time-Bar + FOS questionnaire* — the client answers the approved Time-Bar
     questions first, then the FOS questions.
3. Enter the client name, the 9-digit TMS reference, the lender and the product.
4. For *Time-Bar + FOS* only, also enter:
   - the lender communication or event, and
   - the lender event date.
   These two boxes do not appear for FOS-only, and are not sent.
5. Create the link and send it to the client by your normal channel.

The client must type the same 9-digit reference before any detail is shown.

## What the client sees

| | FOS only | Time-Bar + FOS |
|---|---|---|
| Steps | 5 | 8 |
| Order | intro → FOS → review → confirm → submit | intro → Time-Bar → FOS → review → confirm → submit |

## Links expire

After 72 hours the link stops working and the client is told to ask for a new
one. Create a fresh link; there is nothing to clean up.

## If a client says the link does not work

- Check the reference you entered matches the one they are typing (9 digits).
- Check the link is under 72 hours old.
- Do not edit the link. The token is encrypted and any change invalidates it.

## Receiving answers

Completed questionnaires arrive at `info@moneysolicitor.com` with the subject
**FOS Questionnaire Answers**. The subject deliberately carries no client name,
reference, lender or answer, so open the email to identify the matter. The
record reproduces every question next to the client's answer.

## If a client reports a failure screen

The failure screen means the answers were **not** received — nothing is stored.
Ask them to try again in a few minutes using the same link. They can also leave
a voicemail on 01202 022350 quoting their reference.

## Do not

- Do not send clients the `/admin` address.
- Do not share the staff access key outside authorised staff.
- Do not forward a client's link to anyone else; it is specific to their matter.
